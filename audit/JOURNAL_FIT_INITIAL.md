## Journal requirements (current guide; to be re-verified at submission)

From live checks of the Guide for Authors / Elsevier support (Sep 2026):
- Article type: Research Paper. Word limit ~10,000 (third-party aggregation; verify).
- Abstract: 250 words max.
- Highlights: 3–5 bullets, ≤85 characters each, separate file.
- Graphical abstract: recommended/optional; sparse scientific flow.
- Editable source file (.docx or .tex); PDF not accepted as source.
- Figures: separate files acceptable; limit ~15 for research articles; tables ~5.
- Required declarations: CRediT, competing interests, data availability, generative-AI declaration.
- References: Elsevier numbered style acceptable (we use numbered Vancouver-style).

## Scope-fit assessment (initial)

B&E publishes "building science, engineering, architecture" incl. human–environment
interaction, environmental performance, occupant-centered design, and simulation.
Arguments for fit:
- The independent variable is an environmental boundary condition (gravitational
  field strength) acting on occupant–building-interface mechanics — structurally
  analogous to how B&E treats thermal, luminous, acoustic boundary conditions.
- The objects of study are canonical built-environment elements: doors, seats,
  control interfaces, workstations, circulation.
- Contribution is a design framework/metric, not a vehicle subsystem design.

Risks and mitigations:
- "Space architecture is out of scope": position g* as a continuous environmental
  parameter; Earth (1 g) remains the reference design point; frame habitats as the
  extreme test case of a general rule (interfaces implicitly tuned to g=1).
- "Speculative concept paper": ensure every claim is backed by simulation results,
  sensitivity analysis, and a falsification section.

Verdict: feasible fit, contingent on framing discipline. Recheck at Phase 13.
