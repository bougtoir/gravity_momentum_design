## Desk-rejection risk checklist (from brief §37)

- "too speculative" → mitigated: every claim traces to simulation output; all
  numbers in text are generated from results CSVs; falsification criteria are
  stated and evaluated.
- "space architecture outside scope" → framed as environmental
  boundary-condition mechanics of built-environment interfaces; habitat case is
  positioned as the extreme test of a general g-dependent rule.
- "no validation" → Level A/B external validation documented
  (docs/EXTERNAL_VALIDATION.md); Level C limitation disclosed.
- "obvious mechanics" → novelty is the metric + redesign principle +
  cross-interface test, not Newton's laws; stated.
- "concept paper not research article" → full methods/results/statistics/
  uncertainty/verification sections present.
- "candidate designs unsupported" → mechanical normalization documented;
  negative control and a counter-example variant (lateral slide) retained.
