# HOSTILE_REVIEW.md — Phase 12 internal review

Scored against the 15 challenge questions in the project brief.

1. **"Merely obvious Newtonian mechanics?"** Partially valid — the 1/g scaling of
   capacity is elementary. The contribution is the *cross-interface metric +
   redesign principle*, not the physics. Manuscript states this explicitly.
2. **"Novelty only terminology?"** No: UTI/UAI are operationalized, measured on
   simulations, and shown to separate designs (lateral slide is worse on UTI).
3. **"Cherry-picked designs?"** Five mechanically distinct classes; negative
   control shows invariance; lateral-slide counterexample retained.
4. **"Result trivially follows from 1/g?"** The interaction exists, but *which
   channel* (angular vs transverse) and *where* the threshold sits is
   model-derived, not assumed.
5. **"B&E relevance?"** Framed as environmental boundary-condition mechanics of
   occupant–interface systems. Medium-strong fit; cover letter states it.
6. **"Biomechanics oversimplified?"** Yes — Level-1 rigid body. Stated as a
   deliberate interpretability choice; sensitivity covers parameter ranges.
7. **"UTI/UAI = real stabilization burden?"** Corroborated by corrective-action
   counts and failure rate co-varying; external validation Level A/B only —
   stated.
8. **"Extrapolation below measured range?"** All inferences are model-based;
   experimental agenda given. No Level-C claims.
9. **"Microgravity vs partial gravity confused?"** Terminology kept distinct;
   g* sweeps exclude <0.05.
10. **"Vertical architecture oversold?"** Explicit caveat included; vertical
    door is one candidate, not a universal prescription.
11. **"Aerospace, not building science?"** The object of study is the built
    element, not the vehicle. Rechecked at Phase 13 audit.
12. **"Arbitrary force profiles?"** Task forces anchored to ADA-type/ISO
    operating-force ranges; ±25% MC; sensitivity table shows ordering survives.
13. **"Guidance = hidden contact cost?"** True in general; modeled as
    demand-absorption with reach/confinement penalties recorded in Supplement
    S9 instead of netted away.
14. **"Comparisons mechanically fair?"** Same useful impulse and task time per
    case; Table 2 documents normalizations.
15. **"Operationally naive to astronautics reviewers?"** Sealing, egress,
    actuator failure, suit biomechanics are explicitly out of scope; suit mass
    handled only as sensitivity block.

Outcome: no fatal defect identified; all valid criticisms are either answered
by analysis or disclosed as limitations.
